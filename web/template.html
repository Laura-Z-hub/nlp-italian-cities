01 · Corpus collection

20 Italian cities, taken from it.wikipedia.org

How we built it

  • Source: the opening paragraphs of the Italian Wikipedia article for each of the 20 largest Italian cities.
  • Method: build_corpus.py calls the official MediaWiki API (plain-text extracts, no HTML scraping), with retry on rate limits.
  • Rules: whole paragraphs, 220–300 words per text, headings removed, IPA/dialect brackets after the city name removed.
  • Format: corpus/text01.txt … text20.txt, UTF-8, one paragraph per line.
  • Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Title, URL, access date and word count for each text are in sources.csv.

Why this topic: the texts are factual, well-written, uniform in style and full of named entities (places, people, dates), which suits a search / QA application.

Result in corpus_processed/text01.txt

Lowercase + lemma

Forms such as abitanti and abitante become one lemma, abitante, so the vocabulary shrinks from forms to lemmas.

Italian elisions

dell'omonima is split into dell' + omonima, with lemmas di il + omonimo. Articulated prepositions (del, nel) stay one token.

Why remove stop words?

9 of the 10 most frequent words are function words. They say nothing about which city a sentence describes, so we drop them for search.

03 · Dependencies & entities (displaCy)

Three corpus sentences: what spaCy gets right and wrong

Roma è un comune italiano di 2 743 357 abitanti, capoluogo dell'omonima città metropolitana e della regione Lazio, nonché capitale della Repubblica Italiana.

✔ Roma, Lazio, Repubblica Italiana found as entities; dell' split and lemmatised as di il.
✘ comune tagged ADJ instead of NOUN; the number split into 3 tokens makes abitanti a second nsubj. Our normalisation fixes this.
__SVG1__

Napoli nasce come Partenope, fondata dai Cumani su Pizzofalcone a fine VIII secolo a.C. su base archeologica, sebbene sporadici reperti suggeriscano una datazione più antica.

✔ Napoli, Partenope, Pizzofalcone = LOC; ROOT nasce; fondata → fondare attached to Partenope.
✘ subjunctive suggeriscano gets the non-existent lemma suggeriscare; secolo analysed as obl:agent, but it is a time expression.
__SVG2__

Di origine preistorica, l'abitato venne rifondato dai Romani all'interno dell'ansa del fiume … quando venne occupato dal re germanico Teodorico il Grande.

✔ passive with venire: abitato = nsubj:pass, venne = aux:pass, ROOT = rifondato; Teodorico il Grande = PER.
✘ Romani (a people) and Impero labelled LOC; Grande becomes a separate subject of occupato.

04 · Application area

A search engine for Italian cities

Goal: the user asks a question in Italian (“Quale città ha il porto più importante?”) and the system returns the most relevant sentences from the corpus, with the city and the Wikipedia source.

Why it fits our corpus: short factual texts, one entity (city) per document, rich in names and numbers.

How it works (Phase 1 prototype):

  1. The query goes through the same preprocessing: lowercase, word → lemma (from the corpus lemma table), stop words removed.
  2. Each of the sentences is a TF-IDF vector over lemmas.
  3. Ranking by cosine similarity; top 5 sentences are shown.
